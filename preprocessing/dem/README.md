# DEM preprocessing

The 30 m DEM is built in **`preprocessing/watershed/watershed_data_preparation.ipynb`**,
together with the burned DEM, NLCD land cover and the gauge list — they share one
`py3dep`/`pysheds` pipeline and were not worth splitting apart.

- Source: `py3dep.get_dem(...)` at 30 m, native EPSG:5070, hydrologically conditioned with
  `pysheds` (flow direction, pit filling, inflation).
- Deposited product: `data/static/dem_5070.tif`, and the derived `DEM.npy` (regenerated by `preprocessing/simulation/collect_training_data.py`).
- See `preprocessing/watershed/README.md` for the cell-by-cell breakdown and caveats.
