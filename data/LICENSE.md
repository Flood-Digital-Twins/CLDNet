# Data license

The data in this folder are released under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International license
(CC BY 4.0, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Please cite the paper listed in the top-level README.

They are derived from public-domain US Government sources: USGS 3D Elevation Program (DEM), NLCD 2021 (land cover),
NCEP Stage IV (precipitation) and USGS National Water Information System (gauge stage and discharge).
